What is Indem?

AI-native insurance brokerage and risk intelligence for robotics and AI infrastructure. Starting with robot deployment insurance.

Is Indem an insurer?

No. Indem is an insurance brokerage, not an insurer. Professional brokers review your needs, approach suitable markets and guide placement, policy changes and renewals. Insurers retain pricing authority.

Where is coverage available?

Indem holds insurance brokerage licenses nationwide. Availability, coverage and pricing depend on underwriting, jurisdiction and policy terms.

Does Indem offer financing?

Financing services are in development. Robot deployment insurance comes first. Next: financing for installed fleets, then robot production and fleet buildout.

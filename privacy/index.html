What you share

Our forms request business contact information, a general description of your operation, coverage needs and timing. Your chosen referral source and campaign tags, when present, are included with your inquiry.

How it reaches us

FormSubmit processes the form and sends the inquiry to yudong@indeminsure.com. Indem uses the information to review your request and respond. FormSubmit also handles technical request information under its own privacy policy. This form is an initial contact channel, not a confidential document portal.

Keep sensitive records out

Do not include patient information, government IDs, driver's licenses, VIN schedules, policy documents, raw video or private telemetry. If further records are needed, we will discuss an appropriate way to share them. Sharing operating data for risk modeling requires separate permissions.

Contact

01 / The problem

Insurance was built for machines that stay the same.These do not.

Software changes. Routes change. Tasks change. A PDF assembled once a year cannot explain a machine that learns every day.

Indem keeps the evidence close to the machine, so operators can show what changed and insurance markets can see the risk.

The data advantage

Your operating evidence. A clearer insurance submission.

We are developing a workflow that turns authorized operating records into source-linked evidence for broker and underwriter review. Each pilot starts by confirming what data exists, what it can be used for and what is missing.

  1. 01

    Collect the deployment record

    Operating boundaries, activity and exposure, human oversight, system changes, maintenance, and incidents. We identify available sources and gaps with each pilot operator.

  2. 02

    Build a source-linked Risk Passport

    AI helps structure the records. Broker review keeps corrections, conflicting values, and evidence gaps visible. No record is not the same as no loss.

  3. 03

    Make the case to underwriters

    Documented controls and operating history give brokers evidence to use when seeking better coverage, terms, and pricing. Insurers still assess the risk and set the premium.

A history public data cannot recreate.

We are building a compounding data advantage: operating evidence linked to broker corrections, carrier feedback, and quote, claim, and renewal outcomes as they become available. Public datasets add context; the account-specific history comes from real operations and insurance work.

Pricing and availability remain subject to underwriting. Savings are not guaranteed.

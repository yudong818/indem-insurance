Rental Fleet Insurance Guide: what to prepare before requesting a quote.

01 / The problem

Fleet insurance still runs on reconstruction.

A small account can require the same schedules, loss runs, applications, operating details, and follow-up as a much larger fleet. Owners rebuild the operation for placement, then rebuild it again at renewal or after a loss.

Our customer discovery

Insights from conversations with fleet owners and brokers

Small fleets. Full-size paperwork.

Collecting records, checking details, and chasing missing information take time away from serving the fleet.

Source: Indem founder-led interviews and outreach.

At placement and renewal

Reconstruct the risk

  • Vehicle, driver, and ownership schedules
  • DOT authority or rental operating model
  • Radius, cargo, territory, and vehicle use
  • Current coverage and loss history
During the policy term

Reconstruct every change

  • Vehicle and driver additions
  • Certificates, filings, and endorsements
  • Trip, rental, repair, and loss evidence
  • Emails, portals, and carrier follow-ups

The data advantage

Your fleet's data. A stronger case for better-priced insurance.

We turn your operating data into evidence that helps insurers understand your risk and consider lower premiums. With your permission, we collect the records that show how your fleet is actually run.

  1. 01

    Collect the operating record

    Vehicle schedules, rental dates and status, mileage, maintenance, and loss history where available. Missing records stay marked as missing.

  2. 02

    Build a source-linked Risk Passport

    AI helps structure the records. Broker review keeps corrections, conflicting values, and evidence gaps visible. No record is not the same as no loss.

  3. 03

    Make the case to underwriters

    Documented controls and operating history give brokers evidence to use when seeking better coverage, terms, and pricing. Insurers still assess the risk and set the premium.

A history public data cannot recreate.

We are building a compounding data advantage: operating evidence linked to broker corrections, carrier feedback, and quote, claim, and renewal outcomes as they become available. Public datasets add context; the account-specific history comes from real operations and insurance work.
